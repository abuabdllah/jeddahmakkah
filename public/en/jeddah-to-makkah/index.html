Pickup from anywhere in Jeddah

We pick you up from any hotel or district in Jeddah — the Corniche, Al-Hamra, Al-Rawdah, Al-Balad, Obhur or the south of the city — and drive you to your hotel in Makkah, Al-Aziziyah, or the nearest permitted point to the Haram.

The distance from central Jeddah to Makkah is about 80 km and the drive takes around 60–75 minutes. Curious about exact distances? Read Jeddah to Makkah distance.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Jeddah to Makkah?

From 180 SAR for a sedan from central Jeddah. Pickups from far districts may cost slightly more — we confirm before booking.

How long is the drive from Jeddah to Makkah?

About 60–75 minutes from central Jeddah in normal traffic, longer at peak times.

Do you offer a return trip on the same day?

Yes. The driver can wait or come back at a set time; the price depends on waiting time.
