Landed in ihram: what's the best plan?

Most pilgrims flying in enter ihram on the plane at the miqat and land in Jeddah already in ihram. The usual question is: hotel first, or straight to the Haram? These are the plans we usually arrange:

  • Hotel close to the Haram and check-in open: we drop you at the hotel, you leave your bags, then walk to the Haram.
  • Hotel close but check-in later: most hotels hold luggage at reception before check-in, so you drop it and go for Umrah.
  • Hotel far from the Haram: we go to the hotel first, and you can book a ride from there to the nearest point to the Haram.

Drop-off near the Haram

Roads around Masjid al-Haram are traffic-regulated, and some are closed to private cars at prayer times and in peak seasons. Your driver drops you at your hotel or the nearest permitted point and sends you its location so you know your way back. Travelling with elderly people or a wheelchair? Tell us in advance so we choose the best drop-off point.

Checklist before you land

  • Check your Umrah permit on the Nusuk app if required at the time of travel.
  • Keep ihram clothing in your hand luggage if you'll change on the plane.
  • Send us your hotel name and flight number so we can time your arrival.
  • Pack a small bag with what you need for Umrah and leave big suitcases at the hotel.
  • Save your hotel's address in Arabic on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Can the driver take me to the Haram gate?

Roads around the Haram are traffic-regulated, so we drop you at your hotel or the nearest point permitted for private cars.

Where do I leave my luggage if I go straight to Umrah?

Most Makkah hotels hold luggage at reception even before check-in, so we recommend stopping at the hotel first.

How long from Jeddah Airport to the Haram?

About 60–90 minutes after leaving the airport, depending on terminal, time and traffic.

Is the service suitable for elderly pilgrims?

Yes — tell us in advance about elderly travellers or a wheelchair so we choose the right car and drop-off point.
