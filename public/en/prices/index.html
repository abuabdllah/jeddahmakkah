What affects the price?

  • Car type: sedan is the lowest, then family car and van, then GMC.
  • Exact pickup and drop-off: far districts may add a small amount.
  • Waiting: round trips with the driver waiting are priced by waiting time.
  • Season: prices may change in Ramadan, Hajj and holidays due to high demand.

What's included?

  • Help with luggage
  • Fuel and all road costs
  • Flight tracking

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Jeddah to Makkah?

From 180 SAR from central Jeddah and from 200 SAR from Jeddah Airport, for a sedan.

Are prices fixed?

Your price is agreed before the trip and doesn't change after confirmation, though general prices may be updated in peak seasons.

How do I pay?

Cash to the driver or bank transfer, as agreed when confirming your booking.
