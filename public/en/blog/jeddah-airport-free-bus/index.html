Jeddah Airport to Makkah Bus: Is the Free Shuttle Still Running?

Thousands of pilgrims search every month for the free bus from Jeddah Airport to Makkah. Here's what we know about the service and how to check its current status.

Last updated: 2026-10-16

What was the free airport shuttle?

King Abdulaziz International Airport previously ran a free trial shuttle for Umrah pilgrims from the airport to Masjid al-Haram, on a fixed schedule from Terminal 1. Conditions included wearing ihram or holding an Umrah booking on the Nusuk app.

According to the airport's official account replying to travellers, the trial service ended at the end of Ramadan 1444 AH (2023).

How to check the current status

  • Follow King Abdulaziz International Airport's official account on X
  • Ask your Umrah company whether airport transport is included in your package
  • Check the Nusuk app for services available at the time of travel

Alternatives

If no free bus is running, your options are the Haramain train, taxis and ride-hailing apps, or a pre-booked private transfer from Jeddah Airport to Makkah. Compare them in how to get from Jeddah Airport to Makkah.
