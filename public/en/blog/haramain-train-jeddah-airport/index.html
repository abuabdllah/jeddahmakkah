Haramain Train from Jeddah Airport to Makkah

The Haramain high-speed train is a great option if you travel light. Here's a practical step-by-step guide to using it from Jeddah Airport — and when another option fits better.

Last updated: 2026-10-16

What is the Haramain train?

The Haramain high-speed railway is an electric line linking Makkah, Jeddah, King Abdulaziz Airport, King Abdullah Economic City and Madinah. It's one of the fastest trains in the region and is widely used by pilgrims, visitors and residents travelling between the two Holy Cities.

Where is the train station at Jeddah Airport?

The Haramain station at King Abdulaziz Airport is connected to Terminal 1 and can be reached on foot by following the signs. If you land at the North Terminal or the Hajj & Umrah Terminal, you'll need to get to Terminal 1 first — allow extra time for this.

Step by step: airport to the Haram by train

  1. Clear passport control and collect your luggage.
  2. Walk to the train station in Terminal 1.
  3. Board the train to Makkah — it may stop at Jeddah station on the way.
  4. Get off at Makkah station and take a taxi or bus to your hotel.
  5. From your hotel, head to Masjid al-Haram.

Booking, tickets and luggage

  • Book on the official Haramain train website or app — book early in Ramadan and holidays as trains fill up.
  • There are usually two classes, economy and business; prices vary by class and time.
  • Check the luggage allowance on your ticket — oversized extra bags may not be accepted.
  • Allow time for passport control and baggage claim before choosing your train, especially on international arrivals.

From Makkah station to the Haram

Makkah train station is several kilometres from Masjid al-Haram, so you'll need a taxi or bus to your hotel. At peak times the taxi queue at the station can be long — factor this in when comparing the train with a car.

When is a private car better than the train?

  • Travelling with family, elderly people or children
  • Carrying lots of or large luggage
  • Landing at a terminal other than Terminal 1
  • Landing when there's no train soon
  • Train tickets are sold out

Frequently asked questions

Is there a train from Jeddah Airport to Makkah?

Yes, the Haramain high-speed train has a station connected to Terminal 1 at King Abdulaziz Airport, with service to Makkah station.

Is Makkah train station close to the Haram?

No, it's several kilometres away; you'll need a taxi or bus to reach your hotel.

Can I take big suitcases on the train?

There's a luggage allowance per ticket — check it before booking.

Should I book train tickets in advance?

Yes, especially in Ramadan and holiday periods.
