How far is Makkah from Jeddah Airport?

The distance from King Abdulaziz International Airport to Masjid al-Haram is about 100 km. The drive usually takes 60 to 90 minutes depending on your terminal, time of day and traffic. During Ramadan and holidays expect some extra time near the Makkah entrances.

See our comparison of all ways to get from Jeddah Airport to Makkah — private car, train, taxi apps and buses.

Why a private transfer instead of a taxi or bus?

  • Door to door: from the arrivals hall to your hotel entrance with no changes.
  • Family and luggage together: choose a family car or van to fit everyone.
  • Any hour: landing at 3 a.m.? Your driver will be there.
  • Clear price: you know the cost before booking — no surprises at the gate.
  • English-speaking support on WhatsApp before and during your trip.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Jeddah Airport to Makkah?

Our private transfer starts from 200 SAR for a sedan. The final price depends on the car type, number of passengers and your hotel location. Send your trip details on WhatsApp and we confirm the price before booking.

Where will the driver meet me at Jeddah Airport?

In the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a sign with your name. We send you the driver's phone number and car details on WhatsApp before you land.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number and adjust the pickup time to your actual landing, so you don't need to contact us about delays.

Can you drop me directly at the Haram?

We drop you at your hotel or the nearest point permitted for private cars, as traffic around Masjid al-Haram is regulated at certain times.

Is the service available 24 hours?

Yes, 7 days a week around the clock. Booking a few hours ahead is recommended, especially in Ramadan and peak seasons.

How many passengers and bags fit in each car?

Sedan: up to 4 passengers and 3 bags. Family car and GMC: up to 7. Van: up to 12. Tell us your luggage count and we'll pick the right car.
