Hotel to hotel between the two Holy Cities

Makkah and Madinah are about 450 km (280 miles) apart. By car the trip takes 4.5 to 5 hours on the Hijrah Expressway, including a short stop at a service area.

We drive in both directions — Makkah to Madinah after your Umrah, and Madinah to Makkah if you start your trip with the visit. Your driver collects you from the hotel lobby, loads your luggage, and drops you at your hotel in Madinah or the nearest permitted point to the Prophet's Mosque.

What to expect on the road

  • Departure: we agree the pickup time with you — often after Fajr or after Asr, to avoid the midday summer heat and traffic leaving Makkah.
  • Rest stop: the driver stops roughly halfway for prayer, food and restrooms, and can stop more often for children or elderly passengers.
  • Arrival: the driver calls your hotel or shares his location as you approach; entering the central area takes longer around prayer times.

Madinah to Makkah for Umrah

Travelling from Madinah to perform Umrah? Your miqat is Dhul Hulaifah (Abyar Ali) on the edge of Madinah. The driver stops there so you can bathe and enter ihram, then drives straight to your hotel in Makkah or the nearest point to the Haram.

Jeddah Airport straight to Madinah

Some pilgrims prefer to start with the visit to Madinah: they land in Jeddah and head to Madinah first. Jeddah Airport to Madinah is about 420 km, roughly 4 hours. Share your landing time and we'll meet you in arrivals.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Makkah to Madinah?

From 550 SAR for a sedan, hotel to hotel, depending on car type.

How long is the drive from Makkah to Madinah?

About 4.5 to 5 hours including a short rest stop.

Can we stop on the way?

Yes, the driver stops at service areas for prayer and food, and more often if needed.

Do you also go from Madinah to Makkah?

Yes, and the driver stops at the Dhul Hulaifah (Abyar Ali) miqat if you're going for Umrah.

Can you drive from Jeddah Airport directly to Madinah?

Yes — about 420 km and roughly 4 hours, with meet & greet in arrivals.
