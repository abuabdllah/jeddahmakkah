Al-Hada road or Al-Sail road?

Makkah and Taif are about 90 km apart via the Al-Hada mountain road; the drive usually takes 75–90 minutes. Al-Hada road is one of the most scenic in the Kingdom, with hairpin bends and viewpoints, and arrives straight into the Al-Hada resort area.

The alternative is the Al-Sail road — a little longer but easier and less steep, used when Al-Hada road is closed for maintenance or when your destination is in north Taif. Your driver picks the best road for your destination and road conditions.

Full-day trip from Makkah to Taif

Many families living in Makkah or visiting for Umrah spend a day in Taif, especially in summer when it's much cooler. Book the car for a full day and visit:

  • Al-Hada: mountain viewpoints, the cable car and parks.
  • Al-Shafa: Taif's highest area with fruit farms.
  • Taif rose farms: especially during the spring rose season.
  • Central Taif markets: honey, rose products and local goods.

Then return to your Makkah hotel in the evening. Full-day trips are priced by hours and stops.

Taif to Makkah for Umrah

Coming from Taif for Umrah? Your miqat is Qarn al-Manazil (Al-Sail Al-Kabir) on the Al-Sail road, or Wadi Muhrim on the Al-Hada road. The driver stops at the miqat so you can enter ihram, then continues to your hotel in Makkah or the nearest point to the Haram.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Makkah to Taif?

From 250 SAR for a sedan one way; full-day trips are priced by duration.

How far is Taif from Makkah?

About 90 km via Al-Hada road — 75 to 90 minutes by car.

Do you go from Taif to Makkah for Umrah?

Yes, and the driver stops at Qarn al-Manazil (Al-Sail Al-Kabir) or Wadi Muhrim, depending on the road, for you to enter ihram.

Al-Hada road or Al-Sail road — which is better?

Al-Hada is shorter and more scenic; Al-Sail is easier and less steep. The driver chooses based on your destination and road conditions.
