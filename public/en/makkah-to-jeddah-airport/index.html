When should I leave Makkah for Jeddah Airport?

The drive takes 60–90 minutes. Airlines usually ask you to arrive about 3 hours before international flights and 2 hours before domestic ones, so we recommend leaving your hotel:

  • International flights: about 5 hours before departure.
  • Domestic flights: about 4 hours before departure.
  • Ramadan & peak season: add an extra hour for traffic leaving the central area.

How much is a taxi from Makkah to Jeddah Airport?
